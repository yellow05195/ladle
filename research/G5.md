## 3. How the remainder is distributed

The brief asked for the largest-remainder method. We use **cumulative quota rounding** instead and document why:

- With `C_i = w_0 + … + w_i`: `share_i = floor(tokens·C_i/S) − floor(tokens·C_{i−1}/S)`.
- It telescopes to exactly `tokens`; each share is within ±1 base unit of its exact value (same bound as largest remainder); the leftover units go to the entries where the cumulative fraction crosses an integer — never to "the last one", never dropped.
- It is computable **one entry at a time with O(1) state** (the running `C`), which is what makes `settle_page` possible across several transactions with a strict cursor. Largest remainder requires a global sort of all remainders, i.e. a full extra pass that does not fit a transaction for 500 entries.
- The dependence on entry order affects at most 1 base unit (10⁻⁶ token) per entry.

# G4 — Concentration inside a window

**Date of measurement:** 2026-10-23 (results: `research/data/G4-results.json`, log: `research/data/G4-run.log`)
**Decision: per-entry cap of 20 % of the window total at the time of entry (`max_entry_bps = 2000`), plus an absolute per-entry maximum and an absolute floor for the first entrants.**

## 1. The question

Under the linear law a small player's multiplier is `1 + (r_i − r̄) / ROLL_MAX` with `r̄ = Σ p_j r_j` (p = stake fraction). An entry holding fraction `p_max` of the window moves `r̄` by `p_max · r_max / ROLL_MAX`. The brief asks: how much of a small player's outcome is decided by the biggest entry, as a function of the cap, and to choose the cap with that number.

## 2. Method

```
node --experimental-strip-types sim/src/g4.ts 400000
```

- 400,000 windows **per cap**, 5 to 100 entrants, power-law stakes (Pareto α = 1.5, where whales are common), fixed seed.
- Cap enforced sequentially, exactly as the program does: `a ≤ max(FLOOR, cap · (A_before + a))`, i.e. an entry may not exceed `cap` of the total **including itself** at the moment it enters; the first entrant is limited by the absolute floor.
- Metric: share of a small player's (p < 2 %) multiplier variance explained by the biggest entry's roll, `Var(p_max · r_max / R) / Var(m_small)`, reported (a) on average over all windows and (b) **in the worst case**, i.e. windows where the biggest entry sits at the cap. Analytic worst case for one entry at the cap and everyone else tiny: `cap² / (1 + cap²)`.
- Criterion fixed before running: the loosest cap for which the biggest entry explains **at most 5 % of a small player's variance in the worst case**.

Without a cap, a whale that is half the window decides about **half** of everybody else's outcome variance — their roll matters as much as your own. At 20 % it decides under 4 % in the worst case and under 2 % on average. The next looser cap (25 %) exceeds the 5 % criterion in the worst case.

## 4. Decision and rule

- `max_entry_bps = 2000`: `amount × 10 000 ≤ 2000 × (total_before + amount)`, equivalently an entry may be at most **25 % of what is already in the window**.
- `entry_floor_amount`: below this window total the fraction rule is waived so the window can start (otherwise the first entrant could enter nothing). Set per market at creation; immutable.
- `max_entry_amount`: absolute per-entry ceiling, per market, immutable.
- Splitting a stake over several entries is allowed and is **desirable**: it gives several independent rolls, which lowers both the whale's own variance and everyone else's exposure to them. The cap limits one roll's weight, not one wallet.

## 5. Caveats

- The cap is enforced at entry time against the running total. A big early entry followed by few small ones can still end the window above 20 % of the final total; everyone who entered after it saw it live (entries are public). The guarantee is "no entry exceeded 20 % of the window at the time it entered", and the worst-case metric above is computed on the realised `p_max` at close, so it already includes this effect.
- The 16 % of truncated entries is under Pareto-1.5 stakes, a deliberately heavy-tailed stress case; real stake distributions will truncate less.
